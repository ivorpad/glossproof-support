Your detailing records stay under your control

GlossProof is operated by Ivor Padilla in Spain.

Records created in the app

Customer and vehicle details, job records, service scope, condition notes, before-and-after photos, completion proofs, and exports are stored in GlossProof's app container on your device. GlossProof does not operate an account service, cloud sync service, advertising service, or remote analytics service for that content.

Camera and photo-library access are used only after you choose the related action and grant the required permission. GlossProof keeps a manual path available when an optional capability is unavailable. Production logs use technical state and error codes, not customer names, notes, or media.

Purchases

Apple processes App Store purchases. GlossProof uses RevenueCat to validate receipts and determine whether the Pro entitlement is active. RevenueCat receives purchase history and limited app or device technical information. GlossProof uses an anonymous app user identifier and does not send customer records, vehicle details, notes, photos, or exports to RevenueCat.

RevenueCat's retention and processing are described in its privacy policy.

Sharing

GlossProof sends no detailing content to the developer. Data leaves the app only when you initiate a foreground action such as sharing a completion proof or exporting your data. You see the files before sharing them. The destination you choose is responsible for data after it receives the files.

Retention, export, and deletion

Local records remain on the device until you delete them in the app or remove the app and its data. Original media is not modified in place; edits and exports create derivatives. Existing records remain accessible if a Pro entitlement expires. Files you previously shared or saved must be deleted separately from their destination.

Children

GlossProof is a professional detailing tool and is not directed to children.

Policy changes and contact

This page will be updated before GlossProof changes the categories of data it collects or the purposes for which that data is used. Privacy questions or requests may be sent to ivorjpc@gmail.com.
