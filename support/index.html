Get help with GlossProof

Before sharing a completion proof, review the customer and vehicle details, service scope, condition notes, and every before-and-after pair. If an optional alignment feature is unavailable, use ordinary camera capture and manual alignment.

For purchase issues, open GlossProof's subscription controls to restore purchases or open Apple's subscription management. Existing records stay accessible if Pro expires.

Do not include customer records, vehicle details, photos, access keys, receipts, or other confidential material in an initial support request.

Your controls

  • Review and correct job details before sharing.
  • Use the manual capture and alignment path when optional capabilities are unavailable.
  • Export completion proofs and user-owned app data.
  • Delete individual jobs or all local app data after confirmation.
  • Revoke camera or photo-library access in iOS Settings.
  • Restore or manage App Store subscriptions from the subscription controls.
