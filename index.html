Local-first

Your job records stay under your control

Customer and vehicle details, service scope, condition notes, before-and-after photos, completion proofs, and exports are stored in GlossProof's app container on your device. Data leaves the app only when you choose to share or export it.

Apple processes App Store purchases. RevenueCat validates purchase receipts and the Pro entitlement using an anonymous app user identifier; job records and photos are not sent to RevenueCat.
